RPR1 · Re-Pair word codec

Compression lab

A generic rebuild of the text compression in the 1993 Game Boy KJV: tokenize into words, fold frequent adjacent pairs into grammar rules, store the sorted word list front-coded with a suffix code for common endings, then reserve low byte values as one-byte codes for the hottest symbols. The cartridge reserved 0x55 of those and had roughly 20 suffixes. Paste text, move the knobs, and watch every byte get counted — lexicon and tables included.
