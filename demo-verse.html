RPR1 · YLK1 · read path

Finding John 3:16

Both codecs store the King James Bible as one undelimited run of symbols. Nothing in either stream says where a book, a chapter or a verse begins, and neither stream can be entered in the middle by guesswork: RPR1's tokens are one or two bytes with no way to tell which from the outside, and YLK1's are Huffman codes with no byte boundaries at all. So both containers carry the one thing a reader cannot work out for itself — a table of 66 entries, one per book, which is what the Franklin and cartridge formats of the era had to have and what this repository was missing until now.

This page follows one verse from that table to the words on screen, and counts every address touched on the way. The two codecs pay in opposite places.
