RPR1 · Re-Pair word codec

Compression lab

A generic rebuild of the text compression in the 1993 Game Boy KJV: tokenize into words, fold frequent adjacent pairs into grammar rules, split common suffixes off their stems, front-code the sorted word list, then reserve low byte values as one-byte codes for the hottest symbols. The cartridge reserved 0x55 of them and roughly 20 more for suffixes. Paste text, move the knobs, and watch every byte get counted — lexicon and tables included.
