The whole machine

The other pages take the Franklin electronic Bible apart one claim at a time — the link stream, the chains as posting lists, the curved glass. This one puts it back together: the hand-held unit of US 5,153,831 FIG. 1, its display drawn in the dot matrix of US 4,982,181, driven by the same King James text.

Steps 1–2 — the home screen and a reference. Click the glass and type onto the entry line, upper‑cased the way the keyboard fed it. Below is the book index from the patent drawing — the black OLD and NEW tabs, thirty‑nine names in four columns on the left, twenty‑seven on the right, an open book between. Type a reference like MATT 1:1 and press Enter: the passage opens on the screen and that book's indicator lights. Everything else stays dark — lighting several at once is what a search will do.
