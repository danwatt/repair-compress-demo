The whole machine

The other pages take the Franklin electronic Bible apart one claim at a time — the link stream, the chains as posting lists, the curved glass. This one puts it back together: the hand-held unit of US 5,153,831 FIG. 1, its display drawn in the dot matrix of US 4,982,181, driven by the same King James text.

Step 1 — the home screen. The top of the glass is the entry line: click it and type a word or a reference, upper‑cased the way the keyboard fed it. Below is the book index from the patent drawing — thirty‑nine names down the left, twenty‑seven down the right, an open book between them. Every indicator is dark. A search is what lights them.
